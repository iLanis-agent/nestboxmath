Nestbox math

Drill the hole two millimeters too wide and you have built a starling box by accident. Nestbox math matches the entrance hole to the birds it actually admits, checks the box against the floor and depth they actually use, and counts the boxes a sheet of plywood actually yields. Exact arithmetic, every borrowed number labeled.

Open the calculators

The three tools

  • Hole match - hole millimeters become the species that fit through.
  • Box check - floor and depth become a size verdict by bird.
  • Sheet yield - sheet dimensions become a box count by area.

Labeled norms

Hole ranges (mm): blue tit 25-28, wren 26-30, great tit 28-32, tree sparrow 28-32, house sparrow 32-38, nuthatch 32-35, starling 45-48. Boxes: tits want about a 10-12 cm floor and 20 cm depth; up to 14 cm suits sparrows; 14 and up is starling-sized. Sheet yield is an area estimate - kerfs and layout eat a few. Labeled nest-box references, not rules.
